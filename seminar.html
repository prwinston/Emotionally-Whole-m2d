Emotionally Whole

Formation for Those Who Carry Others
The interior life of the Christian leader — a morning with Winston H.K. Chew

You already know the vocabulary of emotional health. You teach it, preach it, counsel with it. This morning is about the gap between knowing it and living it — the interior work ministry can quietly crowd out. Not another set of techniques, but permission to stop performing wholeness and do the honest work — together with your spouse.

What the morning holds
The Mirror & The Furnace
Seeing yourself truly, and keeping your integrity through the long, unseen seasons.
The Ruins & The Border Crossing
What sustains you when the excitement is gone, and where you go when you are the one running on empty.
The Door
Influence formed by character — and a word for the marriages in the room.
Each session closes with a guided reflection you can carry home.
FOR: Pastors · Elders · Deacons · Missionaries · Full-time Workers — and their spouses
